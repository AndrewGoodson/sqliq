03 / LEARNING WITH GOVERNANCE

Improve the review.
Preserve the boundary.

AEF consolidates repeated control outcomes into evidence-linked learning candidates. Every proposed improvement still needs human review and regression tests.

Only enumerated outcome signals enter the offline learning path. No customer rows, schema contents, credentials or unrestricted prompts. No automatic promotion, model retraining or policy changes.
